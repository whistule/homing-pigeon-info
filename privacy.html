Homing Pigeon: privacy

Homing Pigeon is an internal tool for creating and viewing standard work instructions.

Google data

Homing Pigeon connects to a single Google Drive account owned by the company, solely to store and play training videos uploaded through the app. It uses the drive.file permission, so it can only access files it created itself. It does not read, use or store any other Google data.

Personal information

Homing Pigeon does not collect personal information from the people who view it. A name may be recorded against a video or an edit to show who added it. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Deleting data

Videos can be deleted at any time, from within the app or directly from Google Drive.
